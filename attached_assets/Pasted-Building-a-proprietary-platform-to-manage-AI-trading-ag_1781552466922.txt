Strategy 1: Context-Aware Pairs Trading (StatArb + NLP)
Traditional pairs trading (Statistical Arbitrage) relies on finding two historically correlated stocks (e.g., Visa and Mastercard). When their price spread diverges beyond a certain standard deviation, the algorithm shorts the overperformer and buys the underperformer, betting on mean reversion.

The Problem: Traditional bots get destroyed when a fundamental "regime shift" causes a permanent divergence (e.g., one company announces a massive lawsuit).

The Agentic Edge: Your LLM acts as the ultimate filter.

Step 1 (Quant): A lightweight Python script monitors live market data. When the spread between two co-integrated stocks hits a Z-score of > 2.0, it triggers the Agent.

Step 2 (LLM Context): The Agent rapidly scrapes the last 48 hours of news, SEC filings, and social sentiment for both companies.

Step 3 (Decision): The Agent evaluates: Is there a fundamental reason for this divergence? If yes (e.g., Visa lost a major antitrust case), the Agent aborts the trade. If no (it's just market noise/liquidity driven), the Agent sends a payload to the Robinhood MCP to execute the mean-reversion trade.

Strategy 2: Earnings Call Semantic Alpha (Event-Driven)
This strategy capitalizes on Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD), where stocks tend to trend in the direction of an earnings surprise for days or weeks after the event.

The Problem: Standard algorithms only read the raw EPS/Revenue numbers, entirely missing the nuance of the CEO's forward guidance during the Q&A session.

The Agentic Edge: LLMs excel at semantic analysis.

Step 1 (Ingestion): The Agent is fed the live or newly published transcript of a company's earnings call.

Step 2 (Analysis): You prompt the LLM to act as a forensic linguist. It looks for evasive language, changes in tone compared to previous quarters, or specific hedging words when analysts ask about supply chains or profit margins.

Step 3 (Execution): The Agent generates a "Conviction Score." If the raw numbers were good but the CEO was highly evasive (bearish divergence), the Agent uses the MCP to scale out of the position or initiate a short (when Robinhood opens up options/shorting to agents). If both the numbers and the rhetoric are overwhelmingly bullish, it scales in.

Strategy 3: Thematic Regime Rotation
Robinhood explicitly designed its Agentic accounts to allow for autonomous portfolio rebalancing. This strategy acts as an autonomous macro-manager.

The Problem: Retail investors are typically too slow to rotate sectors when macroeconomic data (CPI, non-farm payrolls, Fed rate decisions) shifts.

The Agentic Edge: The Agent continuously aligns the portfolio with the prevailing macro regime.

Step 1 (Monitoring): The Agent ingests daily macro reports, yield curve data, and geopolitical news feeds.

Step 2 (Thematic Mapping): If the Agent detects a transition into a high-inflation, low-growth regime (stagflation), it autonomously identifies equities that historically perform well in that environment (e.g., consumer staples, specific commodities).

Step 3 (Rebalancing via MCP): The Agent interfaces with the Robinhood MCP to sell high-beta tech exposure and reallocate capital into the identified defensive sectors, respecting the maximum budget allocated to the ring-fenced account.

System Architecture Guidelines for Your Platform
To safely manage these agents on your proprietary website, you should build a distinct separation of concerns:

The Brain (Your Website/Backend): This is where the LLM resides, ingests data feeds (news APIs, financial data), runs the reasoning loops, and logs every decision step for auditability.

The Bouncer (Risk Management Layer): Before any API call is sent to Robinhood, a deterministic, non-AI script checks the trade against hardcoded rules (e.g., "Never allocate more than 10% of the agentic account to a single ticker," "Stop trading if daily drawdown > 5%"). Do not trust the LLM to enforce its own risk limits.

The Hands (Robinhood MCP): Your backend connects to https://agent.robinhood.com/mcp/trading to authenticate and pass the strictly formatted buy/sell instructions.
